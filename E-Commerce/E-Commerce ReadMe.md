E-Commerce Sales Dashboard - Power BI
Project Overview:
This project showcases an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to visualize and analyze key performance metrics of E-Commerce sales. The goal is to provide business leaders with insights into customer spending, sales trends, product performance, and geographical distribution of sales.

Dataset Information:
The dataset comprises transactional data from an E-Commerce platform, including 1000 individual transactions. Key attributes include customer ID, product details, sales quantity, unit price, and country of purchase. The data spans various time frames, allowing for detailed quarterly and monthly analysis.

Key Objectives:

Visualize Total Spend: Understand overall revenue generation through the total spend metric.
Track Customer Engagement: Monitor customer activity by analyzing total customers and invoices.
Analyze Product Performance: Compare sales and spending across different products.
Geographical Insights: Identify key markets by visualizing total spend by country.
Time-Based Analysis: Examine sales trends by quarters and months to identify seasonality or other time-based patterns.
Problem Statement:
The E-Commerce company seeks to analyze and visualize the following metrics:

Quarterly Sales Performance: Track total sales quantity and spending across different quarters.
Product Analysis: Understand which products contribute the most to overall sales and revenue.
Geographical Distribution: Assess which countries drive the most revenue and have the highest customer engagement.
Monthly Spending Trends: Identify any fluctuations in spending patterns over different months.
Dashboard Features:

Summary Cards: Quick view of total spend, total customers, total invoices, total quantity sold, and average unit price.
Sales by Quarter: Visualize total sales quantity and spending trends by quarter.
Product Performance: Analyze total spend and quantity by individual products.
Country-Wise Spend: Map visualization of total spend across different countries.
Monthly Spend Trends: Track total spend by month to identify any significant trends or patterns.
