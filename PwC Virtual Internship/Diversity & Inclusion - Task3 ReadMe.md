# Diversity and Inclusion Dashboard - Power BI

## Project Overview:
This project aims to create a Power BI dashboard that provides insights into diversity and inclusion within 
the organization. The dashboard focuses on key performance indicators (KPIs) related to gender diversity, hiring, 
promotion, performance, and turnover. The goal is to help HR and leadership teams monitor and improve diversity and 
inclusion efforts within the company.

## Dataset Information:

The dataset includes demographic information, promotion data, hiring records, performance ratings, and turnover details.
Key attributes include:

1) Demographics: Gender distribution (men and women).
2) Promotion Data: Number and percentage of employees promoted, segmented by gender.
3) Hiring Data: Percentage of hires by gender.
4) Turnover Data: Number of employees who left the organization, segmented by gender and overall turnover percentage.
5) Performance Ratings: Average performance ratings segmented by gender.
   
## Key Objectives:

1) Monitor Gender Diversity: Track the gender distribution across different levels of the organization.
2) Evaluate Promotion Equity: Assess the fairness in promotion rates between men and women.
3) Analyze Hiring Practices: Monitor gender representation in hiring to ensure diversity in recruitment.
4) Track Turnover Trends: Understand turnover rates and identify potential areas of concern related to diversity.
5) Performance Comparison: Compare performance ratings between men and women to ensure equitable evaluation practices.
   
## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

1) Gender Distribution:
    # of Men: The total number of men in the organization.
    # of Women: The total number of women in the organization.
2) Promotion Equity:
    % Employees Promoted (FY21): The percentage of employees promoted during the fiscal year, segmented by gender.
    % of Women Promoted: The percentage of women who were promoted during the fiscal year.
3) Hiring Diversity:
    % of Hires Men: The percentage of new hires who are men.
    % of Hires Women: The percentage of new hires who are women.
4) Turnover Analysis:
    # of Leavers: The total number of employees who left the organization.
    % Turnover: The overall turnover rate, segmented by gender.
5) Performance Evaluation:
    Average Performance Rating - Men: The average performance rating of male employees.
    Average Performance Rating - Women: The average performance rating of female employees.
   
## Dashboard Features:

1) Gender Distribution Overview: A bar or pie chart visualizing the gender distribution within the organization.
2) Promotion Equity Analysis: A bar chart or percentage gauge showing the promotion rates by gender, with a focus
    on equity.
3) Hiring Diversity Overview: A bar chart or percentage visual displaying the gender distribution of new hires.
4) Turnover Analysis: A bar chart or line graph showing the number of leavers and turnover percentage, segmented by gender.
5) Performance Comparison: A comparative bar chart or scorecard showing the average performance ratings for men and women.
