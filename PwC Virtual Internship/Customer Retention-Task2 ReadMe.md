
# Customer Retention Dashboard - Power BI

## Project Overview:

This project involves the creation of a Power BI dashboard designed for the Retention Manager, with 
the goal of analyzing and visualizing customer retention metrics. The dashboard highlights key 
indicators that help understand why customers leave and what services they use, allowing the manager
to take informed actions to improve retention rates.

## Dataset Information:

The dataset includes detailed information on customers who left within the last month, covering aspects 
like services signed up for, account details, demographic information, and support interaction.

Key attributes include:

1) Services Subscribed: Phone, multiple lines, internet, online security, online backup, device protection,
   tech support, streaming TV and movies.
2) Customer Account Information: Tenure, contract type, payment method, paperless billing, monthly charges,
   total charges, number of tickets opened (administrative and technical).
3) Demographics: Gender, age range, presence of partners and dependents.

## Key Objectives:

1) Identify At-Risk Customers: Highlight customers with high churn likelihood based on their service usage,
   account details, and demographic information.
2) Analyze Service Usage: Understand which services are most and least popular among customers who left, identifying
   potential issues.
3) Monitor Support Interactions: Evaluate the impact of customer support interactions on retention by tracking tickets
   opened in administrative and technical categories.
4) Demographic Analysis: Assess how demographic factors like age, gender, and family status correlate with churn.
   
## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

1) Churn Rate: The percentage of customers who left within the last month, segmented by service type, contract length,
   and payment method.
2) Average Tenure: The average length of time customers stayed before leaving, helping identify patterns in churn timing.
3) Service Usage Analysis: Breakdown of services subscribed by churned customers, highlighting which services are
   associated with higher churn rates.
4) Support Interaction Impact: The number of support tickets opened by churned customers, segmented into administrative
   and technical categories.
5) Demographic Influence: Analysis of churn rates by demographic factors such as age range, gender, and presence of
    dependents.
   
## Dashboard Features:

1) Churn Rate Overview: A KPI visual showing the overall churn rate, with the ability to filter by service, contract
   type, and payment method.
2) Service Usage Analysis: Bar or pie charts visualizing the percentage of churned customers subscribed to each service.
3) Tenure Analysis: A line or bar chart showing the average tenure of churned customers, with filters for service type
   and demographic data.
4) Support Tickets and Churn: A bar chart or heatmap showing the correlation between the number of tickets opened and
   customer churn.
5) Demographic Insights: A set of visuals (bar charts, pie charts) illustrating churn rates across different demographic
   groups, such as age range and family status.
