Sales Performance Dashboard - Power BI
Project Overview:
This project involves the creation of a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and visualize key metrics related to sales performance. The dashboard aims to provide business leaders with a clear understanding of sales trends, profitability, and product performance across different segments and regions.

Dataset Information:
The dataset used in this project includes detailed sales transactions with attributes such as product information, segment data, sales quantity, revenue, profit, and geographical location. The data allows for a comprehensive analysis across various time periods, products, and customer segments.

Key Objectives:

Visualize Total Sales: Understand overall revenue generation through the total sales metric.
Track Profitability: Monitor overall profit and analyze profitability across different segments and products.
Analyze Product Performance: Compare unit sales and profitability by product.
Geographical Insights: Identify key markets by visualizing sales distribution by country.
Time-Based Analysis: Examine sales trends by year and quarter to identify seasonality or other time-based patterns.
Problem Statement:
The Sales Performance Dashboard seeks to address the following business questions:

Overall Performance: How do total sales, profit, and units sold perform across different segments?
Product Analysis: Which products contribute the most to sales and profitability?
Segment Insights: How does profitability vary across different customer segments?
Geographical Distribution: Which countries are the most profitable, and where are the highest sales volumes?
Time-Based Trends: How do sales fluctuate across years and quarters?
Dashboard Features:

Summary Cards: Quick view of total sales, total profit, and total units sold.
Profit by Segment: Visualization of profitability across different customer segments.
Unit Sold by Product: Analysis of the total units sold by each product.
Sales and Profit by Products: Combined view of sales revenue and profitability for each product.
Sales by Year and Quarter: Line or bar chart showing sales trends over years and quarters.
Sales by Country: Map visualization representing total sales across different countries.
